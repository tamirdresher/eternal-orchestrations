Durable execution, explained.

Every time an orchestration awaits a task, the scheduler records an event. If the process dies, the orchestration replays its history and continues exactly where it left off.

  • Your progress is checkpointed after every await.
  • Failures are retried automatically with backoff.
  • State is stored durably — nothing is lost.
